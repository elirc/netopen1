# ASTRA-006: Practice a full review cycle

[Story index](README.md) · [Progress](../PROGRESS.md)

**Stage:** 1 — Onboard and contribute  
**Type:** Collaboration lab · **Estimate:** 1-2 hours  
**Prerequisites:** [ASTRA-005](ASTRA-005.md)  
**Read first:** [Stage lesson](../00-onboarding/04-first-contribution.md)

## User story

As a contributor, I want to respond clearly to review so that the team can improve my change without losing its purpose.

## Starting point and scope

Use ASTRA-005's diff. Pair with a mentor or perform a delayed self-review using the review template; no upstream publication is required.

- [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [markdownlint.yml](../../.github/workflows/markdownlint.yml)
- [.markdownlint.json](../../.markdownlint.json)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Record one correctness concern, one clarity concern, and one positive observation about the diff.
- [ ] Make a focused revision or explain why a suggested change would be inappropriate.
- [ ] Re-run relevant checks after the revision.
- [ ] Deliver a concise final PR description and identify the next learning gap.

## Suggested approach

1. Review the diff after a break and read it as a first-time contributor.
2. Respond to each concern in terms of the intended behavior.
3. Commit the focused correction and update the evidence record.

## Verification and evidence

Submit before/after diff references and a review response. Pass the stage-1 gate only when another person, or your structured self-review, can reproduce setup from your record.

## Hints, in order

1. A review request can be answered with evidence, not only agreement.
2. Do not broaden a one-sentence correction into a complete README rewrite.

## Review conversation

Which review comment changed your understanding, and why? Record the result using the [evidence template](../04-templates/story-evidence.md) and the [definition of done](../00-onboarding/04-first-contribution.md).
